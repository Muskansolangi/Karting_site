Safety First

YOUR SAFETY IS
OUR PRIORITY

We combine the thrill of real motorsport with uncompromising safety standards — so you can push hard with complete confidence.

Safety Helmets Provided

Certified safety helmets are mandatory for all drivers and are provided at the track — free of charge, sanitised after every session.

Trained Race Marshals

Our experienced marshals monitor the track at all times. Full compliance with their signals is strictly required inside the circuit at all times.

Speed-Controlled Karts

Our latest-generation karts are engineered with the highest safety standards. Speeds are calibrated for all skill levels and ages.

Full Racing Flag System

We use the full international flag system — green, yellow, red, and chequered — so drivers are always informed of current track conditions.

Daily Kart Inspections

Every kart undergoes a thorough mechanical inspection before each session. Brakes, tyres and engines verified daily for safe operation.

First Aid On-Site

A fully equipped first aid station is on-site and staffed at all times. Emergency services can reach our venue within minutes.

Track Rules — Please Read Before Racing
Minimum age: 6 years and above for all drivers
Safety helmets are mandatory and must be properly secured at all times
Full compliance with all marshal and supervisor signals is strictly required
No intentional bumping, dangerous driving, or reckless behaviour permitted
No loose clothing, open-toe shoes, or scarves while racing
Pregnant drivers and those with serious medical conditions should not race
